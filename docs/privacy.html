Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

NEAR Publisher is used only by the NEAR team to publish NEAR's own content to NEAR's own accounts (@getnearapp).

What we access

When the account owner connects TikTok, we receive an access token and basic profile information (display name, username, avatar and open ID) for that account only. We use it to show which account is connected and to publish the content the owner chooses.

How we store it

Tokens are stored encrypted and are used only to publish to @getnearapp. We do not collect, sell, rent or share any TikTok user data, and we do not access any other users' accounts, messages, followers or analytics.

Revoking access

The owner can disconnect at any time in TikTok → Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services, which invalidates our access.

Contact

Questions: message @getnearapp on Instagram or TikTok.
